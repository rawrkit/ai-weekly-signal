## 🏗️ Platforms & infrastructure

**Nvidia is in talks to take equity in Perplexity.** The Information reported on August 23 that Nvidia is discussing an investment in a new Perplexity round that would value the company above $30B — up more than 50% from the ~$20B mark set about a year ago. Perplexity's annualised revenue has reportedly climbed past $750M, from under $250M at the start of 2026, helped by Perplexity Computer, its cloud agent for professional tasks. Nvidia has held a stake since 2023, and in July Perplexity committed to running its agent workloads on Nvidia's Vera CPUs. The Information also reported Nvidia weighed a technology-licensing deal as an alternative structure. None of it is confirmed. ([Yahoo Finance / The Information](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-031804276.html), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/61374579/nvidia-perplexity-30-billion-valuation-750-million-revenue-bezos))

**ChatGPT ads go live across Europe.** On August 24 OpenAI switched on advertising inside ChatGPT across 31 European markets — the 27 EU states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland — six months after the US pilot began. Ads appear only on the Free and Go tiers, sit separate from model responses, and launch with no personalisation to stay inside GDPR; Plus, Pro and Enterprise users see none. OpenAI announced the move on August 18. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/21/chatgpt-to-start-showing-ads-across-europe-next-week), [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-ads-business-hits-europe-at-the-six-month-mark/))

*Why it matters:* two moves toward the same goal — not being paid per token. Nvidia keeps extending its balance sheet down the stack, from silicon to data-centre debt and now to equity in the application companies that buy the silicon, so a dollar of AI capex loops back through its own ecosystem. OpenAI is building the other kind of moat: the consumer ad business that funds Google, now running in its largest market outside the US. The model layer is where margin is thinning; both companies are already somewhere else.

## 🧠 Models & capabilities

**Z.ai ships GLM-5.3-Flash under MIT.** On August 26 Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash: a 320B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 18B active per token, natively multimodal (image and video in), a ~1.05M-token context window, weights on Hugging Face, MIT-licensed. API price is $0.15 / $0.50 per million input / output tokens ($0.03 cached input). Z.ai's own numbers show large gains over GLM-5.2 (DeepSWE v1.1 63.4 vs 46.2; AutomationBench 48.8 vs 26.2) and near-parity with Claude Opus 4.8 on a couple of agentic-coding tests (Terminal-Bench 2.1 84.3 vs 85.0; Z.ai Code Bench 29.0 vs 29.5). All of that is vendor-reported and not independently verified. ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-releases-glm-5-3-flash-a-320b-a18b-natively-multimodal-moe-with-a-1m-token-context/), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash))

**Nvidia issues a rare year-ahead revenue guide.** On its August 26 call Nvidia forecast fiscal-2028 revenue growth of roughly 70%, well above the ~44% analysts had modelled, and CFO Colette Kress said Vera Rubin — Blackwell's successor — is "off to a strong start." Full financial detail is in the Money section. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-70percent-growth-forecast-puts-it-on-track-to-be-tech-no-2-company.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-results-q2-earnings/))

*Why it matters:* the open-weights frontier keeps compressing price. A lab outside the US is shipping a model it claims lands within a point of Opus on agentic coding, under the most permissive license, at a few percent of frontier API prices — and giving the weights away. Whether the benchmarks survive independent testing is the standing question of every quiet model week, but the direction is not in doubt. Against that, Nvidia guiding eighteen months out — something it almost never does — is a claim that on the demand side, at least, it can see that far.

## 🎒 Skills & agent tooling

**Skills Radar's weekly scan is all behaviour, no knowledge.** The catalog moved to 87 entries (from 80) with `updated: 2026-08-24`. All seven additions are workflow-behaviour skills rather than knowledge packs: `brainstorming` (a hard approval gate before any implementation), `planning-and-task-breakdown` (holds the agent read-only until the work is a dependency graph), `subagent-driven-development`, `diagnosing-bugs` (forces a reproduction loop before hypothesising), `handoff` (compacts a session into a handoff doc and redacts secrets), `improve-codebase-architecture`, and `principle-fix-root-causes`. Status split is 65 in-kit, 18 new, 3 approved, 1 rejected. The install leaderboard reshuffled — `improve-codebase-architecture` at 775.8K now sits above `grill-me` at 730.1K, followed by `handoff` 654.0K, `diagnosing-bugs` 456.7K, `brainstorming` 336.6K, `grilling` 328.8K. The four counts carried from the last brief (`grill-me`, `grilling` 328.8K, `azure-kubernetes` 322.7K, `code-review` 230.2K) are unchanged; the reshuffle is entirely newly-catalogued skills arriving with existing install bases. The [role-kits](https://github.com/rawrkit/role-kits) repo (23 kits) has had no commits since August 8. ([Skills Radar catalog.json](https://github.com/rawrkit/skills-radar/blob/main/catalog.json))

**Agents turn up in enterprise revenue.** Salesforce reported Agentforce ARR past $1.5B (up ~240% year over year) with 3.2B agentic-workflow actions in the quarter, up 97% sequentially; CrowdStrike tied a record $332.8M of net-new ARR to "agentic AI" demand. Numbers are in the Money section. ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/fy27-q2-earnings/), [Qz — CrowdStrike](https://qz.com/crowdstrike-earnings-q2-fiscal-2027-revenue-arr-guidance-082626))

*Why it matters:* when a scan of what people actually install turns up seven behaviour-modifiers and zero knowledge bundles, it says the bottleneck for agents in real work is not what they know — it is getting them to stop, plan, reproduce and hand off cleanly. The earnings carry the same signal from the other side: the agent features that show measurable revenue are the ones wired into a workflow with guardrails, not the open-ended chat ones.

## 💰 Money

Nvidia, Salesforce, Marvell and CrowdStrike all reported in one week, and the AI trade — shaky after the previous week's bond selloff — got a verdict.

### Nvidia: a $96B quarter and a 70% guide for next year

Nvidia reported Q2 FY2027 (quarter ended July 26) revenue of **$96.2B**, up 18% sequentially and 106% year over year, ahead of the ~$92.2B consensus. **Data Center revenue was $89.0B, up 117%** year over year. GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin were both 75.0%. GAAP net income was $59.7B and non-GAAP net income $54.0B; non-GAAP EPS was $2.22 and GAAP EPS $2.46, against $1.05 a year ago. Operating cash flow was $24.1B, and Nvidia returned $26.0B to shareholders ($19.7B buybacks, $6.0B dividends), with $99.0B left on the repurchase authorisation. ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000073/q2fy27pr.htm))

The guidance is the story. Q3 FY2027 revenue is guided to **$108.0B ±2%** (~12% sequential), above the ~$104B consensus — and it assumes **zero Data Center compute revenue from China**; Kress said the older chips cleared for sale there are running below 1% of Data Center revenue. On the call Nvidia also forecast **FY2028 revenue growth of ~70%**, far above the ~44% analysts had modelled, and said Vera Rubin is "off to a strong start." Jensen Huang framed it as "AI has reached its inflection point." The stock rose about 8.7% the next day, then gave back roughly 4.5% on Friday. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-70percent-growth-forecast-puts-it-on-track-to-be-tech-no-2-company.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-results-q2-earnings/), [Yahoo Finance / Zacks](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-news-aug-28-133400922.html))

### Salesforce: about 40% of the profit wasn't software

Salesforce Q2 FY2027 revenue was **$11.3B, up ~11%** year over year. Non-GAAP EPS printed at **$5.90 — but $2.53 of that was unrealised gains on strategic investments**, roughly $2.7B of which came from its stake in Anthropic (about 45% of an $11.3B investment portfolio). Excluding those gains, operating EPS was about $3.37 versus ~$2.91 a year earlier, ~16% growth. Agentforce ARR passed $1.5B (up more than 240%); Agentforce and Data 360 together reached nearly $3.9B (up more than 210%). cRPO was $33.5B, up 14% in constant currency. Full-year FY2027 revenue guidance was raised $300M to **$46.1–46.4B**. Shares jumped about 22.6%. ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/fy27-q2-earnings/), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/27/salesforce-stock-just-soared-but-investment-gains-delivered-usd2-53-of-its-usd5-90-in-per-share-profit/), [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/salesforces-q2-earnings-beat-by-a-mile-the-guidance-raise-might-matter-more))

### Marvell and CrowdStrike: records and raised guides

**Marvell** Q2 FY2027 revenue was a record **$2.739B, up 37%** year over year and 13% sequentially; non-GAAP gross margin was 58.9% and non-GAAP EPS $0.94 (GAAP $0.33). Data Center revenue was $2.171B — 79% of the total — up 46% year over year. Q3 revenue is guided to $3.15B ±5%, and Marvell again raised its FY2027 and FY2028 revenue targets. Management pointed to an expanded agreement with a major hyperscaler (reported as Google, involving warrants) covering custom inference accelerators, storage controllers, NICs and memory-interface controllers, described in coverage as worth up to $120B over 6.5 years if all milestones are met — the milestone figure is from the earnings call, not the press release. ([Marvell IR](https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1031/marvell-technology-inc-reports-second-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027-financial-results), [Qz](https://qz.com/marvell-record-revenue-ai-chips-guidance-raise-082726))

**CrowdStrike** Q2 FY2027 revenue was **$1.47B, up 26%** year over year; adjusted EPS was $0.31 against a ~$0.29 estimate. ARR reached $5.84B, up 25%, with a record **$332.8M of net-new ARR added (+51%** year over year), which management attributed to "agentic AI" demand. Full-year FY2027 revenue guidance was raised to $5.99–6.01B and the net-new-ARR growth outlook was lifted 630 bps to about 34% at the midpoint. Shares rose roughly 8–12% after the print. ([CrowdStrike IR](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-second-quarter-fiscal-year-2027-financial), [Qz](https://qz.com/crowdstrike-earnings-q2-fiscal-2027-revenue-arr-guidance-082626))

### Anthropic's private mark is doing work on public books

The investment gain in Salesforce's EPS is a preview of what an Anthropic listing would do to a lot of balance sheets. Qz and the Wall Street Journal reported on August 25 that Anthropic is pitching IPO investors on a total market opportunity above $30 trillion; Bloomberg (August 17) put its annualised revenue run-rate past $65B, roughly 7x end-2025, with Q2 revenue reported near $11.6B. Every IPO timing and valuation figure circulating — including a widely reported ~$2T target for an October listing — is unconfirmed by the company and should be treated as reporting, not fact. ([Qz](https://qz.com/anthropic-ipo-investors-30-trillion-market-opportunity-082526), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-ipo-2-trillion-october-largest-ever-spacex/))

*Why it matters:* the week resolved a question the last brief left open — whether the bond selloff meant the AI trade was breaking. Nvidia answered with a bigger sequential guide and a rare year-ahead one, and the tape rewarded it, at least for a day (Nvidia +8.7% Thursday, −4.5% Friday; S&P 500 +0.5% on the week). But watch where the profit came from. Nvidia's is real and cash-generative — $24.1B of operating cash flow. Salesforce's headline EPS was inflated about 43% by an unrealised gain on a private stock. The largest single swing factor across several public companies' earnings this quarter is now the paper value of one private company. That is either the strongest possible vote of confidence in Anthropic or a concentration of risk dressed up as diversification. ([Yahoo Finance / Zacks](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-news-aug-28-133400922.html))
